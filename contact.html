    Join Our Team

    OV³ Lab at the Wangxuan Institute of Computer Technology, Peking University, welcomes applications from prospective postdoctoral researchers, doctoral and master’s students, and research interns. Our research focuses on Multi-Modal AI, Lifelong AI, and Agentic AI.

    We invite motivated candidates with a strong interest in these areas to contact Professor Jiahuan Zhou. Please include a detailed curriculum vitae and a brief statement outlining your research interests, relevant experience, and anticipated start date.

    北京大学王选计算机研究所 OV³ Lab 欢迎有意从事博士后研究、攻读博士或硕士学位以及参与科研实习的申请者加入。实验室主要研究方向包括多模态、终身学习与大模型智能体。

    诚邀对上述领域具有浓厚研究兴趣和积极科研热情的申请者联系周嘉欢老师。来信请附详细个人简历，并简要说明研究兴趣、相关经历及预计入组时间。
